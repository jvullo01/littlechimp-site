Privacy Policy

LittleChimp · Nothing leaves your phone

The short version

Data Not Collected

LittleChimp doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. There are no third-party tracking or advertising tools inside the app, and we don't run any servers for it to talk to. Every sound is made on your phone as you listen, so it plays without an internet connection.

The rest of this page explains the few things that do get saved, and where they stay.

What stays on your phone

To remember how you like things, LittleChimp saves a few things in its own storage on your phone:

  • Your mixes, scenes, favorites and recently played sounds
  • Your bedtime and wake time, if you set them
  • Your settings, like volume ceiling, timers, theme and sky
  • Which app icon you picked, if you switch to one of the alternate icons. iOS applies it on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere
  • Mixes you open from a mix code. A mix code is a short text code you choose to share yourself, for example in a message. Making or entering one never uploads anything; the code only describes the mix
  • How many minutes of tonight's free Lantern Sky preview you've used
  • A general age range, if you choose to share one (see below)

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Like other apps, it can be included in your phone's own iCloud or computer backups, which you control in iOS Settings.

Purchases

LittleChimp is free. The optional unlock is a one-time $2.99 purchase (no subscription) with Family Sharing, sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, Family Sharing and Ask to Buy. We never see your name, email, card or Apple Account details. The app only asks Apple, on your phone, whether the unlock belongs to this Apple Account.

Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Age range

On iOS 26 and later, LittleChimp may ask once for your age range using Apple's Declared Age Range feature. The request is Apple's own screen, and you can say no.

If you share it, the app keeps only a general range (under 13, 13–15, 16–17, or 18 and over) on your phone. It uses this only to choose safe listening defaults, such as the volume ceiling and which tone options are shown. LittleChimp never asks for a birthdate or ID, and the range never leaves your phone. If you say no, or your phone is older, you get the same gentle defaults and nothing is locked.

Children and teens

LittleChimp is made for a general audience and is friendly for teens and families. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it doesn't collect personal information from children under 13 or from teens.

Parents can use Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time to approve purchases and manage app use. Gentle listening defaults, timers and Phone Down are free for everyone.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Nothing leaves your phone → Delete everything on this phone. This removes your mixes, scenes, bedtime and settings, and LittleChimp starts fresh. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.

Your unlock belongs to your Apple Account, not the app's storage, so you can always get it back with Restore Purchases.
