How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy the unlock. Then open Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases in LittleChimp. It's also on the unlock screen. There's no LittleChimp account, so your Apple Account is all you need.

If it says no earlier unlock was found, check that you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that bought it.

Does one purchase cover my family?

Yes. The $2.99 unlock has Family Sharing turned on, so everyone in your Apple Family Sharing group gets it at no extra cost. The person who bought it needs Purchase Sharing turned on in their Family Sharing settings.

Family members usually get it automatically. If not, they can tap Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases.

My child tapped Unlock and it says “Waiting for a parent to approve.”

That's Apple's Ask to Buy. The parent or guardian gets a request on their device. Once they approve, LittleChimp unlocks by itself, even if the app was closed. There's nothing else to do, and the sound keeps playing while you wait. If the request is declined, nothing changes and the free version keeps working.

How does the volume limit work?

Open Settings → Listening safety → Volume ceiling. It sets the loudest LittleChimp will play:

  • Quiet: a low ceiling made for listening all night
  • Comfortable: a gentle ceiling for most rooms and earbuds (the default)
  • Full: no in-app ceiling, available to adults

If headphones are plugged in while the ceiling is Full, LittleChimp eases down to Comfortable unless you're 18 or over. For earbuds, iOS can also limit loud audio under Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Headphone Safety.

A good rule: if you can't easily hear someone talking nearby, it's too loud.

What is Phone Down?

Phone Down is a dark, slow wind-down screen. It hands the night over to the sound so you can put the phone away. Tap Phone Down on Home; the button shows how long it lasts, like “Phone Down · 10 min”. Tap the arrow beside it to change the length to 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes.

The sound keeps playing, your phone's Auto-Lock still works, and nothing is tracked. Phone Down is free for everyone.

How does the free Lantern Sky preview work?

Lantern Sky is LittleChimp's own slow night track. Free users can listen to the first 10 minutes every night. A “night” runs from noon to noon, and paused time doesn't count.

When the preview ends, the sound never stops suddenly and nothing pops up, in case you're already drifting off. It blends over about a minute into Lamplight Tide, a free sound with the same calm feel, and keeps playing. A quiet line offers the unlock if you want Lantern Sky all night.

What's free, and what does the unlock add?

Free includes a full night of sound: Velvet Dark, Lamp Desk for study with its study timer, three Night picks, the Mixer, mix codes, scenes, three themes and more. Every safety tool is free for everyone, always: the volume ceiling, every timer and fade-out, Phone Down, a Still sky whenever you want the background to stop moving, and privacy tools.

The optional $2.99 unlock, paid once, adds Lantern Sky all night, the Thimble Stars music box, the Lamp Desk study pack, many more sounds, soundscapes, tones, themes and skies, your own theme, three more app icons, bigger mixes and unlimited scenes. No subscription, ever.

Does the sound keep playing when my screen is locked?

Yes. You can play, pause, stop and adjust the timer from the Lock Screen and Control Center. Sleep and Night fade out gently after 45 minutes by default. You can change this under Settings → Listening safety → Auto-fade timer.

Do I need the internet?

No. Every sound is made on your phone as you listen, so LittleChimp works on a plane or with Wi-Fi off. Only buying or restoring the unlock needs a connection to the App Store.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings → Nothing leaves your phone → Delete everything on this phone and confirm. This removes your mixes, scenes, bedtime and settings from your phone, and LittleChimp starts fresh like the first time. Nothing is stored anywhere else, so that's all there is.

Your unlock stays with your Apple Account. Use Restore Purchases to get it back.

What is a mix code?

A mix code is a short text code you choose to share, like LC-7K2M-Q9XA. In the Mixer, tap Share as code to make one, or Enter a code to play one a friend sent you. Nothing is uploaded and it works offline: the code only describes the mix, so the app rebuilds it on the other phone. Mix codes are free; if a code uses Premium sounds, the app offers the unlock.

How do I change the app icon?

Open Settings → App icon and tap one: Hanging Chimp (the original, always free), or Moon Listener, Dawn Violet and Deep Teal, which come with the unlock. iOS shows a short message to confirm the change. The choice stays on your phone, and if the unlock is ever refunded, the icon goes back to the Hanging Chimp.

Which devices does LittleChimp work on?

LittleChimp 1.0 is made for iPhone and needs iOS 17 or later. Some features, like sharing an age range through Apple, need iOS 26 or later; everything else works the same.
